        How It All Started

        We Are Hikari

        Building Community Since 2017

        Hikari Cheerleading was founded with a clear vision: to create a space in Rotterdam where safety, technical precision, and inclusivity come first. Named after the Japanese word for "light" (光), we strive to be a welcoming home for every athlete, regardless of their starting point.

        Through years of dedication, growth, and even overcoming the challenges of the pandemic, our focus has remained steadfast on our core values. Today, we are proud to be a vibrant community where passion meets performance — and where we continue to uphold the spirit that earned us the UCA Spirit Stick in 2021.

        What We Stand For

        The Five Values of
        the Hikari Athletes

        We believe the best athletes cultivate the intersection of character, knowledge, and skill.

        Community

        Resilience and discipline are the foundation of everything we do on the mat. Our athletes show up for each other — on match days and on ordinary Tuesdays.

        Safety

        Every session is structured to ensure athletes feel secure and fully supported. All coaches hold current first-aid and cheerleading certifications.

        Inclusivity

        We foster an environment where every athlete belongs and is valued, regardless of experience level, background, or body type. Cheerleading is for all.

        Respect & Accountability

        We ensure every athlete understands the "why" behind every movement, decision, and team role — not just how, but why.

        Being a Cheerleader

        Mastering the correct execution of tasks to contribute to the strength of the collective team. A cheerleader's job is never done in isolation.
